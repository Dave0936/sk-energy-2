SK Energy — About us
#OurStory

We started with underfloor heating. We never stopped caring about comfort.

SK Energy began as an underfloor heating specialist in Dunfermline. As homeowners and developers started asking about low-carbon alternatives, we grew into heat pumps, solar PV, battery storage and EV charging.

That background still shapes how we work. We design properly, explain clearly and stay around afterwards. Every survey, installation and aftercare visit is carried out by our own team, from our offices in Fife and York.
